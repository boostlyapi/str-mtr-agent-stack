# Direct Booking Converter

## Purpose
Identify guests booking through OTAs and implement strategies to convert them into direct booking customers for future stays.

## Workflow
1.  **Identify OTA Bookings**: Scan incoming reservations for OTA-sourced guests (e.g., Airbnb, Booking.com).
2.  **Analyze Guest Profile**: Review guest history and preferences to personalize the conversion offer.
3.  **Generate Personalized Offer**: Create a compelling reason for the guest to book directly next time (e.g., discount, free amenity).
4.  **Automate Outreach**: Send a personalized email or message during or after their stay.
5.  **Track Conversion**: Monitor if the guest uses the direct booking link for future stays.

## Tools
- `ota_identifier_tool.py`: Filters reservations by source.
- `offer_generator_tool.py`: Uses AI to craft personalized direct booking incentives.
- `outreach_automation_tool.py`: Manages the delivery of conversion messages.
